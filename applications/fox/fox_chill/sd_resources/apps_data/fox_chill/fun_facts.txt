The Ferris wheel was invented to rival a famous tower unveiled at an earlier world's fair.
Roller coasters evolved from ice-covered wooden slides built for winter sledding.
The yo-yo has roots as an ancient toy found in cultures across the world independently.
Crayons were originally developed using a mix of charcoal and oil before modern formulas emerged.
Post-it Notes were invented after a scientist accidentally created a weak, reusable adhesive.
The board game Monopoly was based on an earlier game designed to teach the dangers of land monopolies.
Scrabble was invented by an unemployed architect during difficult economic times.
Rubik's Cube was originally created as a teaching tool to help explain spatial geometry.
The dictionary as a structured reference book took centuries to evolve into its modern form.
The world's first computer programmer is widely credited to have been a woman working during the Victorian era.
Early computers were often the size of an entire room and required teams of operators.
The first computer mouse was carved out of wood as an early prototype.
Email existed before the modern World Wide Web was ever created.
The "@" symbol was repurposed for email addresses rather than invented specifically for them.
